01 / ABOUT

The people
behind the pixels.

The current SAIT/CUSAT materials describe SAIT as a student-run association supported by the IT teaching community, with workshops, seminars, projects, magazines and alumni interaction as recurring activities. This page turns that story into a clearer digital system.

01
WHY THIS EXISTS

Make the department easier to navigate.

A student should be able to answer three questions quickly: what is happening, who can help, and what can I do next?

HISTORY
SAIT grew around the idea that learning should continue outside the lecture hall — through workshops, projects, magazines, alumni interaction and student-led activity.

VISION

Curious students.

Make it easier to experiment, collaborate and take ownership of technology.

MISSION

Useful opportunities.

Connect learning with practice through events, resources, projects and people.

VALUES

Open by default.

Share knowledge, celebrate work and leave the next student with better documentation.

VOICE

Student-first.

Less institutional language, more direct paths to information and action.
