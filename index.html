01 / ABOUT

The student layer
of IT at CUSAT.

Designed around the way students actually use a campus website: scan, discover, act, return.

A / STORY

From a student association to a digital campus layer.

The current SAIT site describes the IT Department as a hub for innovation and notes that SAIT supports students through workshops, magazines and technical projects. This redesign turns those same ideas into a clearer, more useful digital experience.
