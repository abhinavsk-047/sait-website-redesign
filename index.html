01 / ABOUT

More than a
student body.

SAIT is the student community of the Department of Information Technology, CUSAT — a space for learning, making, sharing and growing together.

From workshops and magazines to technical projects and campus initiatives, SAIT creates opportunities for students to move beyond the classroom and explore the ever-changing world of technology.
